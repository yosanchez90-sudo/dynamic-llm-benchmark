\section{AI Ethical Considerations}
\label{sec:ethics}

\textbf{Transparency.} Publishing DyMuT's sixty templates as a frozen instance set would contaminate it, the failure it is built to avoid. This work releases the generator and its seeds, so results are reproducible without leaving a memorisable artefact. \textbf{Privacy.} Six of the nine evaluated models are accessed through commercial APIs, so every prompt sent during evaluation is disclosed to those providers, who may retain it for training~\cite{balloccu2024leakcheat}. \textbf{Bias and fairness.} The template grammar and identifier bank reflect one author's judgement of test-generation difficulty and are English-only, while ULT's corpus includes non-English prompts, an uncontrolled language confound between the two benchmarks. \textbf{Safety of execution.} Every generated test file and scraped ground-truth function is executed. Section~\ref{sec:discussion} discloses the actual guarantee: an isolated temporary directory, not a sandbox, defending against accidental side effects.
