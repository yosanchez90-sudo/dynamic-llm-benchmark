\section{AI Ethical Considerations}
\label{sec:ethics}

Transparency. Publishing DyMuT's sixty templates as a frozen instance set would contaminate it, the exact failure it is built to avoid. This work releases the generator and its seeds instead, not a fixed problem pool: results are reproducible without leaving a memorisable artefact behind.

Privacy. Six of the nine evaluated models are accessed through commercial APIs, so every prompt sent during evaluation is disclosed to those providers, who may retain it for training~\cite{balloccu2024leakcheat}. The mitigation is structural: a dynamic instance is discarded and freshly regenerated for every future evaluation, so a leaked run cannot leak the benchmark itself.

Declared AI assistance. An AI coding assistant was used throughout implementation and drafting, under the author's specification and review, per the module's declaration requirement and the fuller account in the supporting report.
