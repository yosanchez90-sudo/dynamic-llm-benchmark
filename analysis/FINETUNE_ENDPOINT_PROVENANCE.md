# Fine-tune endpoint table — provenance

Generated by `scripts/build_finetune_endpoint_table.py` from the corrected
`data/results/finetune_rebuild/epoch_perf_finetune_*_30ep.csv` files (post-
`no_repeat_ngram_size` fix, see `analysis/README.md` / `README_QUARANTINE.md`).
Output: `analysis/finetune_endpoint_table.csv`.

## What this is

The supplementary memorization readout for Comparison C: at the final training
epoch (30, n=150 problems/instantiation), for each of the 6 conditions
(Qwen 1.5B/7B x inst1/bp_own/bp_gpt5), the seen (instantiation 1, the LoRA
training split) vs unseen (mean of instantiations 2/3, held out) baseline-pass
rate, reported two ways:

- **greedy** — the deterministic decode, one value.
- **R=3 sampling mean +- SD** — across `sampling_seed{42,43,44}`, recorded at
  epoch 30 only.

Both `plot_epoch_perf.py` and `plot_epoch_perf_comparison.py` deliberately
filter the sampling draws out of their curves ("a separate endpoint-table
concern") — this table is that concern. It does not replace those scripts'
existing `--gap-csv` output (`analysis/figures/epoch_perf_{qwen1_5b,qwen7b}
_bp_gap.csv`, greedy-only); it adds the sampling-replicate view alongside it.
The greedy `gap_pp` values in both places agree (e.g. Qwen 1.5B bp_gpt5:
52.7pp either way).

## Headline verdict: bp_gpt5, Qwen 1.5B, n=150

| split | greedy | sampling mean +- SD |
|---|---|---|
| seen (inst1) | 54.0% | 53.3% +- 2.4 |
| unseen (mean inst2/3) | 1.3% | 1.7% +- 0.6 |
| **gap** | **52.7pp** | **51.7pp** |

Largest memorization gap of all 6 conditions (next largest: Qwen 7B bp_gpt5,
68.0pp greedy — same condition, larger model). `bp_gpt5` = fine-tuned on the
GPT-5-generated leaked-twin (PLT) data; the near-total seen/unseen split
(>50pp at both sizes) is the expected signature of a model that memorized the
static benchmark's specific problems rather than learning a generalizable
skill — consistent with the contamination-resistance hypothesis this
experiment is designed to test. `inst1` (no static leakage) stays within
+-2.6pp at both sizes, i.e. no detectable seen/unseen gap, as expected for the
non-contaminated control.

API/instruct-model runs used for Comparison A/B, not the fine-tuned
checkpoints. Confirmed still gated on supervisor confirmation as of this
